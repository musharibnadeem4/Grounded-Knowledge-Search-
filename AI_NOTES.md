For the longest session I set the rules first: a keep/reject rule written before any run (at least two more questions right on the tuning set, zero access-control leaks, no more than 0.5 s extra latency), a held-out split I would not look at until the end, and no commits. One of the noteable mistakes AI made: its first hybrid search (BM25 plus embeddings) returned 50 results instead of the k I asked for, so the context the LLM read was bigger than designed and the experiment looked like a gain of three questions. I noticed when the contexts held about twelve chunks although I had asked for eight, found the bug, re-ran, and the gain was zero. I kept the wrong run, marked it superseded, and added a regression test. Since then I check that a result can be explained before I believe it. Other things I noticed during development.
